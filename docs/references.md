## Electronic clinical quality measures (eCQI Resource Center)

| # | Source | What it supports here |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | eCQI Resource Center, *CMS165v13 Controlling High Blood Pressure* (2025 performance period). <https://ecqi.healthit.gov/ecqm/ec/2025/cms0165v13> | Age 18-85. Essential hypertension "starting before and continuing into, or starting during the first six months of the measurement period". Numerator: most recent BP adequately controlled (<140/<90). Exclusions (ESRD/dialysis/transplant, pregnancy, hospice, palliative care, frailty/advanced illness, long-term nursing home). Representative-BP guidance: lowest systolic and lowest diastolic on the same day; exclude acute inpatient and ED readings; no reading counts as not controlled. Steward: NCQA. |
| M2 | eCQI Resource Center, *CMS165v14* (2026). <https://ecqi.healthit.gov/ecqm/ec/2026/cms0165v14> | Confirms the population, denominator and numerator are unchanged from v12 to v15. |
| M3 | eCQI Resource Center, *CMS122v13 Diabetes: Glycemic Status Assessment Greater Than 9%* (2025). <https://ecqi.healthit.gov/ecqm/ec/2025/cms0122v13> | Age 18-75 with diabetes and a visit. Numerator: most recent glycemic status assessment (HbA1c or GMI) >9.0%, or missing, or not performed. Lowest result if several on one date. Lower score is better. Exclusions (hospice, nursing home, frailty/advanced illness, palliative care). Steward: NCQA. |

## Synthetic data generator

| # | Source | Note |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Synthea, open-source synthetic patient generator. <https://github.com/synthetichealth/synthea> | Synthea was evaluated first because it is the preferred generator. The official `synthea-with-dependencies.jar` (master-branch-latest release) ran under Java 21: `-p 200 -s 42 -cs 42 -r 20260101 --exporter.years_of_history=3` took 69 s and produced 578 MB of FHIR for 254 patients. Extrapolated to 5,000 patients, that is about 14 GB and about 30 min per run, which is incompatible with a repository under 20 MB and fast deterministic CI. The project therefore uses its own seeded Python FHIR R4 generator (`synth/`); see `docs/limitations.md`. |
